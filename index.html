1. 我們收集哪些資料

本 App 不需要註冊或登入，不會收集你的姓名、電子郵件、電話、地址或帳號等個人資料。你在 App 內輸入的價格、數量與折扣、最低手續費等設定，只儲存在你自己的裝置上，不會上傳到我們的伺服器。

本 App 的免費版會顯示 Google AdMob 提供的廣告。為了投放與衡量廣告，Google Mobile Ads SDK 可能收集並使用下列資料：裝置識別碼、概略位置（由 IP 位址推估）、廣告與產品互動資料，以及當機與效能診斷資料。這些資料由 Google 依其政策處理，詳見 Google 隱私權政策與 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資訊。

本 App 不會要求「追蹤」授權，也不會使用廣告識別碼（IDFA）追蹤你。購買「移除廣告」後，App 內不再顯示廣告。

3. App 內購買

App 內購買（移除廣告、請喝咖啡）由 Apple App Store 處理。付款資訊由 Apple 處理，我們不會取得你的信用卡或付款資料。「是否已移除廣告」的狀態與贊助次數只儲存在你的裝置上，並以 Apple 的交易紀錄驗證。

4. 資料分享

除上述 Google AdMob 之外，我們不會與任何第三方分享資料，也不會出售資料。

5. 兒童隱私

本 App 並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

Stock Trade Calculator (台股買賣試算) Privacy Policy

Effective: October 18, 2026 · Developer: Taiwan Innovation App

1. Data we collect

The app has no sign-in and does not collect your name, email, phone number, address or account information. The prices, quantities, discount and minimum-fee settings you enter stay on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

2. Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use device identifiers, approximate location (derived from the IP address), ad and product interaction data, and crash and performance diagnostics. Google processes this data under its own policies; see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The app does not ask for tracking permission and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) to track you. After you buy "Remove ads", no ads are shown.

3. In-app purchases

In-app purchases (Remove ads, Buy me a coffee) are processed by the Apple App Store. Apple handles payment information; we never receive your card or payment details. Whether ads have been removed, and the number of coffees you have bought, are stored only on your device and verified with Apple's transaction records.

4. Sharing

Other than Google AdMob as described above, we do not share data with third parties and we do not sell data.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
